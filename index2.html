🎓 Why We Celebrate Today

Teacher's Day is celebrated on September 5th to mark the birth anniversary of Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan (Born: Sept 5, 1888). A renowned scholar and the 2nd President of India, he believed teachers should be the best minds in the country. When asked to celebrate his birthday in 1962, he requested it be observed as Teacher's Day instead, to honor the educators who shape our future.
